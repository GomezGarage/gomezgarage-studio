ABOUT

Near Bucharest, Romania.

Gomez Garage Studio is a one-person operation: design, print, post-process and ship, all done in the same workshop.

The Story

Why this exists

Gomez Garage started as a car project. Studio grew out of it — the same obsession with precision and finish, applied to objects instead of a car. Every product here is designed from scratch, not downloaded, not copied, and printed in the same workshop it's designed in.

Process

CAD → Print → Post-process → Ship

01

CAD

Every part is modeled from scratch and checked for print tolerances.

02

Print

Printed on industrial FDM printers with heated chambers.

03

Post-process

Sanded, deburred and inspected by hand for a consistent finish.

04

Ship

Packed and shipped from Romania across the EU and UK.

Equipment

The setup

Industrial FDM printers with heated chambers, run and calibrated in-house for consistent, warp-free prints at scale.
